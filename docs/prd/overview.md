# Product Overview

## Product Vision

A banner-creation tool for small retail business owners who sell on Russian marketplaces (Ozon, Wildberries, etc.). The tool lets a non-designer produce professional, marketplace-ready product banners in minutes — no freelancers, no design skills required.

## Goals

- **Cut cost:** eliminate per-banner freelancer fees.
- **Cut time:** go from product idea to finished banner in under 5 minutes.
- **Raise quality:** every banner looks polished and marketplace-compliant out of the box.
- **Unlimited iterations:** try different colors, text, and layouts at zero marginal cost.

## Target Users

**Primary:** Independent store owners (40-60 age range) who resell imported goods on Russian marketplaces. Low technical literacy, no design background. They know their products well but struggle with visual presentation.

## Scope

### In (MVP)

- Banner creation from a product photo URL + short product description
- Curated set of professional templates optimized for marketplace listings
- Color scheme and text customization
- Export in marketplace-compatible formats and dimensions

### Out (MVP)

- Video banners or animated content
- Direct marketplace integration / auto-upload
- Bulk generation for entire catalogs
- AI-generated product descriptions
- Print-format banners (in-store posters, price tags)

## Cross-Cutting Concerns

- **Language:** UI in Russian. Banner text in Russian.
- **Accessibility:** large controls, simple flows — the user is not tech-savvy.
- **Performance:** banner preview should feel instant; final render under 10 seconds.
